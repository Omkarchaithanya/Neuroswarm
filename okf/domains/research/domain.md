# Research Domain

## Guidance

- Prefer web-search for discovery, browser MCP for deep page extract
- Cite sources; keep OKF institutional claims separate from Mem0 facts
- Use cascade tiers: triage (T1) → synthesis (T2) → deep reason (T3) when confidence low
- Respect cost budget policy for long-form runs
